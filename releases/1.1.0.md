## 新增

- **格式转换勾选**：转换页每个项目前有复选框，只有勾选的项目才会转换；顶部显示「已选 X/N」，可一键全选或全不选。
- **展开面板保持按钮**：面板右下角新增「悬停」与「收起」两个按钮——按「悬停」面板一直保持展开，直到点「收起」；按「收起」立即收起。

## 改进

- 展开面板在触发展开后悬停 5 秒再自动收起；悬停期间鼠标移开不再提前收起，「悬停」与热键固定仍然优先。
- 通知监听收紧为固定白名单：只监听微信、QQ、TIM、企业微信、钉钉、飞书、Teams、Slack、Discord、Telegram、WhatsApp、LINE、Skype、Zoom、腾讯会议、Notion，以及 Outlook、Foxmail、Thunderbird、网易邮箱大师、QQ 邮箱共 21 个聊天与邮件程序；其他软件与系统通知一律不显示。
- 胶囊提醒动态作用于整个胶囊（弹跳或音爆，按设置选择），并循环保持直至点击胶囊；音爆的发散改为整个胶囊轮廓的回声，不再是白色圆圈。
- 点击通知胶囊直接拉起对应软件的窗口，并把该程序的全部通知标记为已读。
- 收起栏的律动条与文字固定在正中，两端边距一致；切换时间样式时收起栏长度随之自适应。

## New

- **Conversion selection**: each row on the convert page carries a checkbox, and only the checked ones convert; the toolbar shows "X of N selected" with one-click select-all or none.
- **Panel hold buttons**: two buttons at the panel's bottom-right — 「悬停」(hold) keeps the panel open until 「收起」(collapse) is pressed, and 「收起」closes it at once.

## Improved

- A triggered panel now stays open for five seconds before collapsing on its own; moving the pointer away no longer closes it early, while the hold button and the hotkey pin still take precedence.
- Notification watching is now a fixed whitelist of 21 chat and mail programs (WeChat, QQ, TIM, WeCom, DingTalk, Feishu, Teams, Slack, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, LINE, Skype, Zoom, Tencent Meeting, Notion, Outlook, Foxmail, Thunderbird, NetEase Mail Master, QQ Mail); notices from anything else are no longer shown.
- The capsule reminder animates the whole capsule (a bounce or a sonic burst, as set) and keeps looping until the capsule is clicked; the sonic burst now expands as an echo of the capsule itself instead of a white ring.
- Clicking the capsule brings the source program's window up and marks all of that program's notices read.
- The collapsed bar keeps its rhythm strip and text centred with equal margins at both ends, and the bar's length follows a clock-style change.

## Other

- The About page now names the open-source licence (MIT License).
- Build leftovers and temporary files were cleaned; README, the licence file and version notes were added for the open-source release.
