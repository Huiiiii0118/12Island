## 新增

- **通知中心**：新增页面，集中显示 QQ、微信、邮箱与系统通知。收到通知后，收起的小岛会一直显示这条提醒，直到打开通知页看过为止。
- **剪贴板历史**：记录复制过的文字、图片与文件，可搜索、收藏、加备注、删除，点击条目即可复制回剪贴板。
- **格式转换**：拖入或选择文件，图片、文档、表格、文本、音频、视频与 PDF 全部在这台电脑上转换，不联网，也不需要安装任何软件；音视频用的是随程序附带的 FFmpeg。

## 改进

- 三个新页面沿用面板原有的外观、配色与动效，可与原有页面自由排序、调整尺寸、开关显示。
- 页面渲染改为整体构建后一次替换：某次绘制出错时，页面保持上一次的样子，不再出现空白。
- 移除了外部版本检查与项目链接，软件不再联网检查更新。

## New

- **Notifications**: a page for QQ, WeChat, mail and system notices. An unread notice stays on the collapsed island until the notification page has been opened.
- **Clipboard history**: text, pictures and files that were copied, with search, stars, notes and deletion; click an entry to put it back on the clipboard.
- **Conversion**: drop or pick files and convert pictures, documents, spreadsheets, text, sound, video and PDF on this machine, with no internet and nothing to install; the FFmpeg the program carries does sound and video.

## Improved

- The three pages follow the panel's own look, colors and motion, and can be reordered, resized and switched off like the others.
- A page is now built aside and swapped in whole, so a failed render keeps the previous page instead of leaving it blank.
- External version checks and project links were removed; the app no longer asks the internet for updates.
- The program name and author details no longer appear in the interface.
- A macOS-style dark look: a near-black panel, a faint rim of light and the blue accent, shared by the settings window.
- The panel keeps one theme in three modes (system, dark, light); theme extensions, theme files and the theme editor are gone.
- Every window takes an opacity setting (60%–100%).
- The system readings take their colors from the panel theme instead of per-reading pickers.
- The music page turns full control of NetEase, KuGou and Apple Music into one switch.
- A collapsed notice is an iPhone-style card: app icon, bold sender, one line of text and the time.
- The settings sidebar is two levels: Basics, Appearance, Features, System, About.
- scripts/Version-Bump.ps1 sets the next version from how much of the source changed.
