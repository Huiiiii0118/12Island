## 新增

- **邮件类通知点击**：点击收起栏的邮件胶囊会查找本机邮件客户端（Outlook / Foxmail / Thunderbird / 网易邮箱大师 / QQ 邮箱）的窗口并将其唤起；找不到客户端时，消息自动标记已读并取消提醒，不再额外启动邮件程序。

## 改进

- **滚动文字窗口加宽**：播放音乐时，收起栏中间滚动显示的歌名/歌词可见窗口提升到 10 个字符宽（随面板缩放同步）。
- **封面移入中间块**：收起栏的专辑封面移到律动条左侧，与文字、律动组成一个整体块，两端边距更均衡。
- **自动收起更自然**：展开面板改为「指针离开后 5 秒」自动收起——指针停留在面板上时不再倒计时；原「悬停」按钮更名为「展开」。
- **面板透明度**：展开面板的不透明度跟随设置中的「外壳透明度」。
- **复制提示自动消失**：剪贴板页「已复制回剪贴板」的提示 5 秒后自动清除，恢复常规操作指引。
- **邮箱状态更明确**：邮箱通知未开启或未填写邮箱地址时，页面状态栏直接说明原因，不再显示"正在监视邮箱"。

## 其他

- 移除律动条颜色选择器：律动条固定使用面板主题文字色，旧配置文件仍可正常加载。
- 默认值调整：默认样式改为胶囊、时钟默认显示日期、窗口最大化行为改为常规。
- 应用内「更新内容」窗口只显示最近 3 个版本的日志。

## New

- **Mail notice clicks**: clicking the mail capsule on the collapsed island looks up the local mail client's window (Outlook / Foxmail / Thunderbird / NetEase Mail Master / QQ Mail) and brings it to the front; when no client is found, the notices are marked read and the reminder ends — no mail program is started instead.

## Improved

- **Wider scrolling text**: while music plays, the visible window for the scrolling title/lyric in the collapsed row is now ten characters wide (scaling with the panel).
- **Cover joins the middle block**: the collapsed row's album cover now stands at the rhythm bar's left, one block with the text and the rhythm, keeping the margins at both ends balanced.
- **More natural auto-collapse**: an expanded panel now closes five seconds after the pointer leaves it — no countdown while the pointer rests on the panel; the former "悬停" (hover) button is renamed "展开" (expand).
- **Panel opacity**: the expanded panel's opacity now follows the settings' shell opacity.
- **Copy hint fades**: the clipboard page's "copied back to the clipboard" hint clears itself five seconds later, restoring the usual guidance.
- **Clearer mail status**: when mail notifications are off or no mailbox address is set, the page's status line says so directly instead of claiming the mailbox is being watched.

## Other

- The equalizer colour picker is removed: the equalizer always takes the panel theme's own text colour, and older settings files still load.
- Defaults adjusted: the capsule style by default, the clock shows the date by default, and the maximised-window behaviour is the regular one.
- The in-app "what's new" window now lists only the three most recent versions.
